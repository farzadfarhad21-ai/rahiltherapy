About Raheleh

Raheleh Avinipour is a licensed general psychologist (member of the Psychology and Counseling Organization of Iran) with a master's degree in general psychology and a clinical focus. She is trained in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and completed advanced Schema Therapy training at Tehran University of Medical Sciences. She has over 15 years of clinical experience and more than 3,000 therapy hours.

She works only with women. This is a deliberate choice: in her sessions, nobody has to explain or justify what it is like to be a woman holding several roles at once, carrying family expectations, or rebuilding a life after migration.

  • Location: Dubai, United Arab Emirates — in-person sessions in Dubai
  • Online: worldwide via Zoom, WhatsApp video or Google Meet
  • Languages: Farsi (Persian) and English

How I can help

Anxiety & panic

Constant worry, racing thoughts and panic attacks, treated with evidence-based CBT.

Depression & emptiness

Low mood, loss of meaning, or feeling empty despite outward success.

OCD

Intrusive thoughts, checking, Pure O and religious OCD, treated with ERP. Read the OCD guide (Farsi).

Relationships & attachment

Repeating patterns, fear of abandonment, or difficulty with closeness — with schema therapy.

Migration & identity

Homesickness, culture shock, loneliness abroad and guilt toward family back home.

Self-esteem

Perfectionism, harsh self-criticism and impostor feelings.
