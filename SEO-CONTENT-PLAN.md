# SEO Content Plan & 3-Month Editorial Calendar
## rahiltherapy.com — Persian RTL Psychology Therapy Site

**Prepared:** ۱۲ خرداد ۱۴۰۵ (June 2, 2026)
**Owner:** Raheleh Evinipour, Clinical Psychologist (Dubai)
**Primary Goal:** Drive organic Persian search traffic → establish E-E-A-T → convert visitors to free first session booking
**Secondary Goal:** Build topical authority across 8 service pillars

---

## Executive Summary

rahiltherapy.com is a 100% Persian RTL static site targeting Farsi-speaking populations globally, with explicit focus on the Iranian diaspora. The site has a working technical foundation (10 articles, 8 service pages, daily blog cron) but lacks a strategic content architecture. The 10 existing articles are topic-rotated broadly — they touch each service area once but do not build topical clusters, internal-link equity, or keyword depth.

This plan delivers:

1. A **content gap analysis** identifying 14 high-demand Persian mental health topics the site does not cover.
2. A **Persian keyword research framework** organized by search intent (informational, navigational, commercial, transactional), with primary, secondary, and long-tail variants for each cluster.
3. A **12-week editorial calendar** with 12 cornerstone/depth/authority articles — full brief per piece (title, target keyword, meta, word count, outline, internal links).
4. An **E-E-A-T content plan** to upgrade author bio, showcase credentials, structure testimonials, and demonstrate expertise through trust signals.
5. A **content promotion strategy** tuned to Persian audiences on WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, and Aparat.

Persian keyword research is constrained — there is no equivalent of Ahrefs/Semrush for Persian. The framework below is built on:

- Persian mental health forums (NiniSite, مشاوره, روان‌شناسی, Reddit /r/Iran)
- Google Suggest / People Also Ask (when accessed from Persian IPs)
- Cultural frequency: which topics Iranian clinicians most commonly address
- Commercial value: queries that precede booking behavior

## 8. Content Anti-Patterns to Avoid

For YMYL content, the Sept 2025 QRG flags these as low-quality signals — avoid at all costs:

1. **No first-person fabricated experiences** — only share case material with full anonymization OR general clinical patterns.
2. **No vague reassurance** — "نگران نباشید، همه چیز خوب می‌شود" is a low-quality AI marker. Be specific.
3. **No clickbait** — Persian audiences are sophisticated; they detect it. Use direct, evidence-based hooks.
4. **No keyword stuffing** — keep keyword density <2% for primary. Use synonyms (e.g., روان‌درمانگر، روانشناس، درمانگر، مشاور).
5. **No identical structure across articles** — vary outlines, especially intros. Repetitive templates are a Sept 2025 AI-content red flag.
6. **No claims without sourcing** — for any clinical claim, cite the source (APA, WHO, NHS, PubMed).
7. **No duplicate content** — every cornerstone must offer unique value not found in the existing 10 articles.
