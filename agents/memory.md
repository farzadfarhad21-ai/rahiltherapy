## Content Formats That Get Engagement

### High-Performance Formats
1. **Personal letter / first-person narrative** — "این مقاله را برای مراجعان آینده‌ام نوشتم" — highest E-E-A-T signal, strongest trust building
2. **Case study (anonymized)** — "داستان یک مراجع" — drives WhatsApp shares, high emotional engagement
3. **5-signs / checklist style** — "۵ نشانه که وقت مراجعه رسیده" — viral potential, high saves
4. **Technique-focused** — "۳ تکنیک فوری برای آرام کردن ذهن" — practical value, shared in WhatsApp groups
5. **FAQ / What to expect** — "اولین جلسه چگونه است" — pre-conversion, high intent

### Low-Performance Formats
- Pure definition articles ("X چیست") without practical angle
- Long pillar articles (>2000 words) that don't break into scannable sections
- Articles without clear CTA or next step

**Engagement Pattern:** Telegram posts with plain URLs (not markdown links) get more clicks. Instagram carousel format outperforms single-image posts for Persian mental health content.

## Mistakes to Avoid

### Content Mistakes
1. ❌ **No keyword stuffing** — Persian audiences are sophisticated. They detect it immediately. Keep keyword density <2%.
2. ❌ **No vague reassurance** — "نگران نباشید، همه چیز خوب می‌شود" is a low-quality AI marker. Be specific.
3. ❌ **No identical structure** — Repeating same template across articles is an AI-content red flag. Vary outlines.
4. ❌ **No claims without sourcing** — For clinical claims, cite WHO, APA, NHS, PubMed. Uncited claims damage E-E-A-T.
5. ❌ **No duplicate content** — Every article must offer unique value. Existing 10 articles are already indexed.

### Technical Mistakes
1. ❌ **Don't deploy with broken booking form** — Direct revenue loss. Check before every deploy.
2. ❌ **Don't change profile image without testing** — Hero image affects trust. Test on small group first.
3. ❌ **Don't use markdown links in Telegram** — Use plain URLs. Markdown links open in-app browser, plain URLs open external browser.
4. ❌ **Don't add articles without updating sitemap** — Google won't index new content.

### Strategy Mistakes
1. ❌ **Don't publish cornerstone articles without internal links** — Each needs 6-8 internal links to other articles/service pages/booking
2. ❌ **Don't reduce daily automation below 3x/week** — Freshness signals matter for Persian search
3. ❌ **Don't skip Telegram message after article generation** — Each article needs distribution, not just publication
