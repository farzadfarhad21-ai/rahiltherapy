## Rules

- Hook must be attention-grabbing, first line only, no emoji
- Value lines should be insightful, practical, relatable
- CTA mentions "جلسه اول رایگان" (first session free) and WhatsApp number
- Hashtags: mix of Persian and English, 10-15 total, include #روانشناسی #سلامت_روان and topic-specific tags
- Tone: warm, professional, empathetic, encouraging
- Never mention specific prices or fees
- No content about suicide, self-harm, or crisis situations
