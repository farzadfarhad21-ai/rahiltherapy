## Rule 1: Persian Mental Health Audience is Sophisticated

Persian-speaking audiences (Iranian diaspora + Iran) are highly educated about mental health topics. They can detect:
- AI-generated content that lacks clinical depth
- Keyword stuffing or unnatural phrasing
- Vague reassurance without evidence

**How to handle:** Write with clinical specificity. Use proper terminology (CBT, طرحواره, etc.) naturally. Cite sources (APA, WHO, PubMed) for clinical claims. Your content should sound like it was written by someone with 15+ years clinical experience — because it was.

**Anti-pattern to avoid:** "نگران نباشید، همه چیز خوب می‌شود" — this is an AI-content red flag.

---

## Rule 2: Emotion Opens Reach, Practicality Drives Bookings

The best-performing content strategy for @rahiltherapy:

- **Top-of-funnel (reach):** Emotional vulnerability topics — افسردگی، اضطراب، روابط
- **Bottom-of-funnel (bookings):** Practical topics with clear CTAs — اولین جلسه، هزینه، مشاوره آنلاین

**How to handle:** Start articles with emotional hook (relatable struggle). End with practical next step (booking). The Telegram message should mirror this: hook line → value → plain URL → CTA.

**Anti-pattern to avoid:** Pure "چیست" definition articles without practical angle. These get good snippets but low engagement.

---

## Rule 3: Telegram = Plain URLs, Instagram = Carousel

Different channels need different treatment:

| Channel | Format | CTA | Notes |
|---------|--------|-----|-------|
| Telegram | Title + 3-line summary + plain URL | WhatsApp link | Plain URLs open in external browser (higher CTR) |
| Instagram | Carousel (5-7 slides) | Link in bio | Save-able content gets more reach |
| WhatsApp Status | Quote card + visual | Article URL | Persian audiences share heavily on WhatsApp |

**Anti-pattern to avoid:** Using markdown links `[text](url)` in Telegram — they open in-app browser, not external. Use plain URLs.

---

## Rule 4: Every Article Needs Internal Links

Cornerstone articles must have 6-8 internal links to:
- 2+ other articles (depth/authority)
- 1-2 service pages
- booking.html (always)
- about.html (for E-E-A-T)

Depth articles link back to at least 1 cornerstone.

**How to handle:** When creating an article, always include internal links section in the outline. Don't publish until internal linking is complete.

**Anti-pattern to avoid:** Publishing articles with zero internal links — damages SEO crawlability and topical authority.

## Rule 5: Before Deploying, Verify Three Things

Before any deploy, check:
1. **Booking form works** — Broken booking = direct revenue loss. Test on staging if possible.
2. **sitemap.xml updated** — New articles must be added within 5 minutes of going live.
3. **Telegram message queued** — Every article needs distribution, not just publication.

**How to handle:** The automation script handles #2 and #3 automatically. For manual work, add to checklist before deploy.
